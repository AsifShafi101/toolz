    What a UK invoice must include

    • A unique invoice number (we number them for you: INV-0001, INV-0002…)
    • Your business name and address, plus the customer's name and address
    • The invoice date, plus a clear description of what you supplied and when
    • The amount owed. If you're VAT registered, also show your VAT number and the VAT for each rate

    Check GOV.UK for the full rules, especially if you're a limited company or VAT registered.

    Get paid faster

    • Put your bank details on every invoice.
    • Set a clear due date; 14 or 30 days is common.
    • Send the invoice as soon as the work is done.

    Questions

    Is this invoice generator really free?

    Yes. You can make unlimited invoices with no watermark and no sign-up.

    Where is my data stored?

    Only in your browser on this device. Your business details are remembered so you don't retype them. Nothing is uploaded to us.

    How do I save it as a PDF?

    Click 'Download PDF / Print' and choose 'Save as PDF' as the printer. On a phone, use the share or print option.

    I'm not VAT registered. What do I choose?

    Set each line's VAT to 'No VAT'. The VAT column then disappears from the invoice.
