    Millilitres to UK fluid ounces

    Convert millilitres to UK fluid ounces instantly. 1 millilitre = 0.0352 fl oz. Type a number to convert it, or use the table of common values below.

    ml

    Formula: fl oz = ml × 0.0352 (or ml ÷ 28.4131)
