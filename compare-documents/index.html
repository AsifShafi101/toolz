    Compare documents

    Compare two versions of a contract, essay or report and see every word added or removed. It works with PDF, Word (.docx) and plain text, all on your device.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Good for

    • Checking what a client or landlord changed in a contract
    • Comparing essay drafts or policy versions
    • Proofreading edits before you sign

    Green text was added in the new version and red text was removed.

    Questions

    Are my documents uploaded?

    No. Text is extracted and compared in your browser, which is ideal for confidential contracts.

    Can it compare scanned PDFs?

    Scans are images. Run them through Image to text (OCR) first, then paste the text here.
