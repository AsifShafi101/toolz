Website screenshot tool

Take a screenshot of any website as it looks on a desktop, laptop, tablet or phone. You can capture the visible screen or the full page.

Download JPG

Your screenshot will appear here.

Uses

  • Check how your website looks on phones and tablets
  • Save evidence of a web page or advert as it appeared
  • Add website images to presentations, portfolios and reports

Screenshots are taken in a real browser by our server (Cloudflare). Only public websites can be captured, so pages behind a login won't work.

Questions

Why did the screenshot fail?

Some websites block automated visitors or take too long to load. Try again, or try the site's home page.

Is there a limit?

The free service has a daily capacity. If it's busy, please try again a little later.
