    Invoice data extractor

    Drop in PDF invoices and get the supplier, invoice number, dates, net, VAT and total in a table. Correct anything that's wrong, then download a CSV for Excel or your accounts software.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    What it finds

    Supplier, invoice number, invoice date, due date, net amount, VAT, total, currency, the supplier's VAT number and bank details (sort code, account number and IBAN). Every invoice is laid out differently, so check the values and click any cell to fix it before exporting.

    Scanned (photo) invoices contain no text. Use Image to text first.

    Questions

    Does it work with Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent?

    Download the CSV and use your software's bank or bill import, or keep it as a record for your accountant.

    Is it safe for confidential invoices?

    Yes. Invoices are read in your browser and never sent anywhere.
