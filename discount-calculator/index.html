    Discount calculator

    Work out a sale price after a percentage discount, including 'extra % off' deals, or find what percentage off a price is.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Calculated on your device. Figures are rounded to the nearest penny.

    Tip: '25% off, then an extra 10% off' is a 32.5% discount in total, not 35%. The second discount applies to the already-reduced price.

    Questions

    How do I work out 20% off?

    Multiply the price by 0.8. For example, £50 × 0.8 = £40.
