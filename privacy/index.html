    Privacy policy

    Last updated: 2 November 2026

    The short version

    The free tools on etoolbox.co.uk run entirely in your web browser. What you type into them, including Wi-Fi passwords and contact details, is not sent to us or stored by us.

    What we collect

    • Emails you send us: if you contact us or join early access, we keep your email so we can reply. We never sell it.
    • Basic visit statistics: we may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) that count page views without cookies or tracking you across sites.
    • Usage counts and feedback: we count page visits and tool uses (just a number per tool, with no cookies or personal data). If you send feedback, we store your 👍/👎, your optional comment and a scrambled, daily-changing code used only to stop spam. It can't identify you.
    • Uploaded logos: if you upload a logo to the email signature generator, we store that image so it can appear in your emails. Email hello@etoolbox.co.uk to have it removed.
    • Payments: if you buy Pro, payment is handled by our payment provider (Stripe). We don't see or store your card details.
