    Webcam, microphone & speaker test

    Check your camera, microphone and speakers before a Teams, Zoom or job interview call. You'll see the camera's resolution and a live microphone level, and can play a left/right speaker test.

    Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Fixing common problems

    • Permission denied: click the camera icon in the address bar and allow access.
    • Camera in use: close Teams, Zoom or other apps using it.
    • Mic bar doesn't move: check you've picked the right microphone and that it isn't muted on the device or headset.

    Questions

    Is my video recorded or sent anywhere?

    No. The preview and test recording stay on your device and disappear when you leave the page.
