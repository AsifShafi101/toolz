Barcode generator

Create EAN-13, UPC-A and Code 128 barcodes for products, stock labels and shelf edges. Download them as PNG or SVG. The check digits are calculated for you.

Always test-scan before printing a batch.

Which barcode do I need?

  • Selling in shops or on Amazon? You need a real EAN-13 number registered to your business through GS1 UK. You can't just make up the number, but you can use this tool to draw the barcode once you have it.
  • Stock control, shelf labels, internal SKUs: use Code 128, which works with letters and numbers.

Questions

Do I need to buy barcodes?

For retail products, yes. Retailers expect GS1-registered numbers. For your own stock system, any Code 128 barcode works.
