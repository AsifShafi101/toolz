    WhatsApp QR code generator

    Customers scan and a WhatsApp chat with your business opens, with an optional ready-typed message. It's great for orders, bookings and quotes.

    Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    UK numbers starting with 0 are converted to +44 automatically. Include the country code for other countries.

    Colours & download size
    Your QR code will appear here as you type.

    Made in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored.
    Free codes never expire.

    Printed it already and need to change the link?

    Pro dynamic QR codes let you change where a code points at any time, and show you how many scans each one gets.

    Ideas

    • Takeaways: "Hi, I'd like to order…" on flyers and windows.
    • Trades: "Hi, I'd like a quote for…" on your van and leaflets.
    • Salons: "Hi, I'd like to book an appointment" at the till.

    Questions

    Does the customer need my number saved?

    No. The code opens a chat with you directly, even if they don't have you as a contact.

    Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

    Yes. It works with both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business numbers.
