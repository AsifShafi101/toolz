    Meeting time finder

    Find a meeting time that suits everyone across time zones. See all the local times side by side, with working hours highlighted and the best slots listed.

    Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
    Working hoursEarly / eveningNightWorks for everyone

    The first time zone is your base. Each column is one hour of your day. Daylight-saving changes are handled automatically for the date you pick.

    Questions

    Does it handle British Summer Time?

    Yes. Times are worked out for the exact date, including BST and daylight saving elsewhere.
