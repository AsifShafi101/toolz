    Favicon generator

    Make a complete favicon set for your website from a logo or from a letter or emoji. Get favicon.ico, PNG icons, the Apple touch icon, Android icons, site.webmanifest and the HTML to paste, all in one ZIP. Free and private.

    Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    How to add a favicon to your website

    1. Unzip the files and upload them to the root folder of your site, next to your home page.
    2. Paste the HTML snippet into the <head> of every page.
    3. Reload your site. Browsers cache favicons, so you may need a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R).

    Google shows your favicon next to your site in search results. It needs to be at least 48×48 pixels, which the ICO file includes.

    Questions

    What size should a favicon be?

    Browsers use 16×16 and 32×32 in tabs, Google wants at least 48×48, Apple uses 180×180 and Android uses 192×192 and 512×512. The ZIP includes all of them.

    Can I use an emoji as a favicon?

    Yes. Type an emoji in the letters box. It's drawn with your device's emoji font.

    Do I still need favicon.ico?

    Modern browsers can use PNG, but favicon.ico in your site's root folder is still the most reliable option, so it's included.
