    Word counter

    Count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs as you type, with reading and speaking time. It's great for essays, ads, bios and social posts.

    Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Common limits

    • Google Business Profile description: 750 characters
    • Instagram bio: 150 characters
    • Meta description (for Google results): about 150–160 characters
    • X (Twitter) post: 280 characters (standard accounts)

    Platforms change their limits from time to time, so check before you publish.

    Questions

    Is my text saved or uploaded?

    No. Counting happens in your browser and nothing is stored.

    How is reading time worked out?

    It's based on an average adult reading speed of about 238 words per minute and a speaking speed of 150 words per minute.
