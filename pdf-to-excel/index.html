      How to convert a PDF to Excel

      1. Choose a PDF with tables, such as a bank statement, invoice, price list or report.
      2. Check the preview to see how the columns were detected.
      3. Click Download spreadsheet and open it in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.

      Works best with

      • PDFs created by software (online banking, accounting tools, Word or Excel exports)
      • Clear tables with one row per line

      Scanned PDFs are pictures of text, so run them through Image to text (OCR) first. For invoices, our invoice data extractor pulls out totals, VAT and dates.

      Questions

      Is my bank statement uploaded?

      No. The PDF is read in your browser, so your financial data never leaves your device.

      Why are some columns merged or split?

      PDFs store text positions, not real tables, so columns are worked out from where the text sits. Complex layouts can need a little tidying in Excel.

      Does it work with Google Sheets?

      Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, or choose CSV and use File → Import.

      Is it free?

      Yes. No sign-up and no limits.
