    Loan repayment calculator

    See your monthly repayment, the total cost and the interest on a personal loan, car finance or business loan.

    How it's worked out

    We convert the APR (annual percentage rate) to a monthly rate and use the standard repayment formula. Your lender's figure may differ by a few pence because of fees and rounding.

    Questions

    What's a good APR?

    It depends on your credit history and the amount. Compare the representative APR from several lenders, and check eligibility tools that don't affect your credit score.
