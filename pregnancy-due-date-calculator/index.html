    Pregnancy due date calculator

    Work out your baby's due date from the first day of your last period, your conception date or an IVF transfer date. See how many weeks pregnant you are, your trimester and the key dates for scans, maternity leave and more.

    Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    This is an estimate. Your midwife will confirm your due date at your dating scan.

    Useful? Share it free:WhatsAppXLinkedInFacebookEmail

    How is a due date calculated?

    A pregnancy is counted as 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of your last period, not from conception. With a typical 28-day cycle, conception happens around two weeks after your period starts, so you're already "2 weeks pregnant" when you conceive. If your cycle is longer or shorter than 28 days, the due date moves by the difference, which this calculator adjusts for.

    • From conception: due date = conception date + 266 days.
    • IVF: 5-day transfer + 261 days, or 3-day transfer + 263 days.

    The NHS pregnancy timeline

    In the UK you'll usually have a booking appointment with a midwife by 10 weeks, a dating scan between 11 and 14 weeks, and a mid-pregnancy scan between 18 and 21 weeks. The key dates list shows these for your pregnancy, plus UK maternity deadlines. You can add them all to your phone's calendar.

    This calculator gives general information, not medical advice. Contact your midwife or GP with any questions, and see the NHS pregnancy guide.

    Questions

    How accurate is a due date?

    It's an estimate. Only about 1 in 20 babies is born on the due date itself; most arrive between 37 and 42 weeks. Your dating scan gives the most accurate date.

    How many weeks pregnant am I?

    Enter the first day of your last period, and the calculator shows how many weeks and days pregnant you are today, counted the way midwives count.

    When do I need to tell my employer?

    In the UK, by the end of the 15th week before the week your baby is due. The calculator shows this date. Check GOV.UK for the full rules on maternity leave and pay.

    Is my information stored?

    No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
