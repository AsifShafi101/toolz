    How much can I save?

    Phone photos are often 3–5 MB. Resizing them to 1600 px wide at 75% quality usually brings them under 300 KB with no difference you can see on screen. That makes a big difference to how fast your website loads.

    Which format should I use?

    • WebP: the smallest files, supported by all modern browsers. Best for websites.
    • JPG: works everywhere. Best for photos you'll email or print.
    • PNG: for logos and graphics with transparency (use the converter).

    Questions

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Compression happens entirely in your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

    Why is the file sometimes bigger?

    Some images are already well compressed. If we can't make the file smaller without resizing, we keep your original.
