    Email header analyser

    Paste an email's headers to see the route it took, any delivery delays, and whether it passed SPF, DKIM and DMARC. It also flags signs of spoofing or phishing.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
    How do I find the headers?
    • Gmail: open the email → ⋮ → Show original → Copy to clipboard.
    • Outlook: open the email → … → View → View message details.
    • Apple Mail: View → Message → All Headers.

    What the results mean

    • SPF: was the sending server allowed to send for that domain?
    • DKIM: was the message signed by the domain and left unchanged?
    • DMARC: did it pass the domain owner's anti-spoofing policy?

    A failure on a message claiming to be from your bank or HMRC is a strong sign of phishing. Don't click links in it.

    Questions

    Is it safe to paste headers?

    Headers are analysed in your browser and not sent anywhere. They contain addresses and server names, not the message body.
