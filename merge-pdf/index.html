    Merge PDF files

    Combine several PDFs into one document in the order you choose. It's free, needs no sign-up, and your files never leave your device.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

      Result

      Your file will appear here, ready to download.

      How to merge PDFs

      1. Choose two or more PDF files (or drag them in).
      2. Use the ↑ ↓ arrows to put them in order.
      3. Click Merge PDFs and download the combined file.

      Private by design

      Most PDF websites upload your files to their servers. etoolbox processes them in your browser, so contracts, invoices and ID documents never leave your device.

      Questions

      Is there a file size limit?

      There's no fixed limit. Very large files (hundreds of MB) depend on your device's memory.

      Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

      Not yet. Remove the password first, then merge.
