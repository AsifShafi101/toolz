When to use each format

  • JPG: photos. Small files that work everywhere, but no transparency.
  • PNG: logos, screenshots and graphics. Supports transparency, but files are larger.
  • WebP: websites. Small files, with transparency support.

Questions

Can I convert WebP to JPG?

Yes. Choose JPG and select your WebP files.

What happens to transparent backgrounds when converting to JPG?

JPG doesn't support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background colour you choose (white by default).
