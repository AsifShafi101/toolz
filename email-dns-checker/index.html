    SPF, DKIM & DMARC checker

    Check that your business email is set up to reach inboxes and resist spoofing. We look up your MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records and explain what to fix.

    🌐 Uses our server to look up public DNS records

    Why it matters

    Since 2024, Gmail and Yahoo require senders to have SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up properly. Without them, your invoices and quotes are more likely to land in spam, and criminals can more easily send emails pretending to be you.

    • SPF lists the services allowed to send your email (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Mailchimp…).
    • DKIM digitally signs your emails.
    • DMARC tells receivers what to do with emails that fail. Start with p=none, then move to quarantine.

    Questions

    I use Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. Where do I add these?

    In your domain's DNS settings (wherever you bought the domain, or Cloudflare). Both providers' admin centres show the exact records to add.
