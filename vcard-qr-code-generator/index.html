    Where to use a contact QR code

    • On the back of business cards, so people save you in one tap.
    • On your van, shop window or market stall banner.
    • On email signatures, quotes and invoices.

    Only fill in what you want to share. The more details you add, the denser the code gets, so keep it short for small prints.

    Questions

    What happens when someone scans it?

    Their phone shows your details and offers to add you to their contacts.

    Can I update my details later?

    A free code can't be edited, so you'd make a new one. A Pro dynamic code can point to details you update at any time.
