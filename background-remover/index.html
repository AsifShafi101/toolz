    Background remover

    Remove white or plain backgrounds from product photos, logos and signatures. Download a transparent PNG or swap in a new background colour. Your image stays on your device.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Tip: click any leftover background area in the preview to remove it too.

    Best results

    • Works best on photos with a plain or white background: product shots, logos, signatures, scanned stamps and icons.
    • Increase tolerance if bits of background remain, and lower it if parts of your subject disappear.
    • For busy backgrounds (people outdoors, rooms), an AI cut-out tool will do better. That's coming to etoolbox Pro.

    Questions

    Is my image uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser.

    How do I make a transparent signature?

    Photograph your signature on white paper in good light, then upload it here and download the PNG.
