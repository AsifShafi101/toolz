    Questions

    What's the difference between free and Pro QR codes?

    Free (static) codes contain your link directly and can never change. Pro (dynamic) codes point to a short etoolbox link that you control, so you can change the destination at any time and see how many scans it gets.

    What happens to my Pro codes if I cancel?

    We'll give you plenty of notice and a chance to move your links before anything stops. Your free codes are never affected.

    Can I cancel any time?

    Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time.
