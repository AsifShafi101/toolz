    Savings & compound interest calculator

    See how your savings could grow with regular deposits and compound interest. It's useful for ISAs, savings accounts and business reserves.

    Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Calculated on your device. Figures are rounded to the nearest penny.

    Useful? Share it free:WhatsAppXLinkedInFacebookEmail

    This assumes the rate stays the same and leaves out tax. Interest within your Personal Savings Allowance or an ISA is tax-free.

    Questions

    What is compound interest?

    It's interest earned on your interest. Over time, your balance grows faster than with simple interest.
