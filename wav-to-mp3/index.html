    How to convert WAV to MP3

    1. Choose your WAV file, or drag it onto the box.
    2. Pick a quality. 128 kbps suits most things; use 320 kbps for music.
    3. Press Convert to MP3. The MP3 downloads when it's done.

    Good to know

    WAV files are uncompressed, so an hour of CD-quality stereo takes about 600 MB. The same hour as a 128 kbps MP3 is about 55 MB, small enough to email, upload or keep on your phone.

    Questions

    How much smaller will my file be?

    At 128 kbps, roughly 10 times smaller than a CD-quality WAV. At 320 kbps, about 4 times smaller.

    Is my file uploaded?

    No. It's converted in your browser, so it never leaves your device.

    Is there a length limit?

    No. The file is read in pieces, so 1–2 hour recordings work fine. Longer files just take a little longer.
