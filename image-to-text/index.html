    Image to text (OCR)

    Copy the text out of screenshots, photos of documents, receipts and scans. Paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V or choose an image. Text recognition runs on your device.

    Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    The text reader (about 11 MB) downloads once, the first time you use it.

    Tips for accurate results

    • Screenshots work best, because the text is perfectly sharp.
    • For photos, fill the frame with the page, keep it flat and use good light.
    • Crop away anything that isn't text.

    Questions

    Is my image uploaded?

    No. The text recognition engine (Tesseract) runs entirely in your browser.

    Does it work with handwriting?

    Only neat, printed-style handwriting. It's designed for typed text.
