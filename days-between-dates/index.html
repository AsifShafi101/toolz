Days between dates calculator

Count the days and working days between two dates (excluding England & Wales bank holidays), or add and subtract days, weeks, months or working days.

Calculated on your device. Figures are rounded to the nearest penny.

Uses

  • Invoice due dates (e.g. 30 days from today)
  • Notice periods and contract end dates
  • Counting working days for projects and holidays

Working days exclude weekends and England & Wales bank holidays from 2025 to 2028. Scotland and Northern Ireland have some different bank holidays.

Questions

Does it count the start date?

No. It counts the days from the start date to the end date. Tick 'Include the end date' to count both.
