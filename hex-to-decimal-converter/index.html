    How to convert hex to decimal

    Each hex digit is worth 16 times the one to its right. For 2F: 2 × 16 + 15 = 47. The letters A–F stand for 10–15.

    How to convert decimal to binary

    Keep dividing by 2 and write down the remainders from bottom to top. For 13: 13→6 r1, 6→3 r0, 3→1 r1, 1→0 r1, so 13 = 1101.

    Questions

    Can it handle very large numbers?

    Yes. It uses exact whole-number maths, so numbers of any length convert without rounding.

    What is two's complement?

    It's how computers store negative numbers. Type a negative decimal (e.g. -5) and choose the word size to see its two's complement binary.

    Can I use 0x or 0b prefixes?

    Yes. 0xFF, 0b1010 and 0o17 are accepted, and spaces or underscores between digits are ignored.
