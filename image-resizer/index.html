    Popular sizes

    • Instagram post: 1080 × 1080 (square) or 1080 × 1350 (portrait)
    • Facebook cover: 851 × 315
    • Website hero image: 1600–2000 wide
    • Product photos: 1200–2000 on the longest side

    Platforms change their recommended sizes from time to time, so check their help pages for the latest.

    Questions

    Will resizing reduce quality?

    Making images smaller keeps them sharp. Making them much bigger than the original will look blurry.

    Can I resize lots of images at once?

    Yes. Select up to 30 images and they'll all be resized with the same settings.
