      Why etoolbox?

      Most PDF and image websites upload every file you give them to their servers. etoolbox works differently: the tools run inside your web browser, so contracts, payslips, ID scans and customer photos stay on your device. It's quicker too, because there's nothing to upload.

      • Actually free. No sign-up, no watermarks, and QR codes that never expire.
      • Made for UK businesses. Pounds, UK VAT, 2026/27 tax rates, HMRC mileage rates, UK dates and +44 phone numbers.
      • Everything in one place. PDF tools, an AI background remover, an invoice generator, calculators, QR codes and website tools.

      Questions

      Are the tools really free?

      Yes. Every tool on etoolbox is free to use with no sign-up and no daily limit. An optional Pro plan adds extras such as your logo on invoices and dynamic QR codes.

      Are my files uploaded?

      No, for almost every tool. PDFs, images, invoices and calculations are processed in your browser, so the files never leave your device. The few tools that need a server, such as the website screenshot tool, say so clearly on the page.

      Do the tools work on a phone?

      Yes. etoolbox works in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Chromebook. There's nothing to install.

      Are the UK tax calculators up to date?

      Yes. The take-home pay, mileage and VAT calculators use the 2026/27 UK rates and thresholds, including Scottish income tax bands.

      Do you add a watermark to my files?

      No. etoolbox never adds its own branding or watermark to your PDFs, images or invoices.
