Why your rate is higher than an employee's

As a freelancer you don't get paid for holidays, sick days, admin, marketing or time spent finding clients. You also pay your own equipment, software and insurance costs. Most freelancers can only bill 20–30 hours in a typical week.

How it's calculated

Hourly rate = (target income + business costs) ÷ (working weeks × billable hours per week).

The income you enter is before tax. Remember to set aside money for Income Tax and National Insurance, and for a pension.

Questions

What should I enter for billable hours?

Use only the hours you can actually charge to clients. 20–30 hours a week is common once you allow for admin, emails and finding work.

Should I charge by the hour or by the day?

Day rates or fixed project prices often work better for both sides. Use the hourly figure as your minimum and build your quotes from it.
