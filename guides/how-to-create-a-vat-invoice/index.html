    How to create a VAT invoice in the UK

    Updated 6 November 2026 · etoolbox guides

    If you're VAT-registered and you sell to another VAT-registered business, you normally have to give them a VAT invoice, usually within 30 days of the sale. Your customer needs it to reclaim the VAT, so getting it right matters to both of you. Here's what HMRC says it must include, in plain English.

    Do you need to charge VAT?

    Only VAT-registered businesses can charge VAT or issue VAT invoices. You must register once your taxable turnover goes over £90,000 in any rolling 12 months (the threshold for 2026/27). Below that you can register voluntarily. If you're not registered, send a normal invoice and don't add VAT.

    What a full VAT invoice must show

    1. A unique invoice number that follows on from your last one (INV-0041, INV-0042…).
    2. Your business name, address and VAT registration number.
    3. The invoice date, and the time of supply (tax point) if it's different.
    4. Your customer's name (or trading name) and address.
    5. A description of each item clear enough to identify what you supplied.
    6. For each item: the quantity, the unit price excluding VAT, the VAT rate and the amount excluding VAT.
    7. Any discount you've given.
    8. The total excluding VAT and the total VAT, shown in pounds sterling.

    Prices in a foreign currency are fine, but the VAT amount must also be shown in sterling.

    Simplified invoices for £250 or less

    If the total including VAT is £250 or less, you can give a simplified VAT invoice instead. It only needs your name, address and VAT number, the time of supply, a description of the goods or services, and for each VAT rate the total including VAT and the rate charged. You don't need the customer's details.

    Common mistakes

    • Charging VAT when you're not registered. That's not allowed.
    • Reusing or skipping invoice numbers. Keep one unbroken sequence.
    • Showing one total with no VAT breakdown. The VAT must be shown separately.
    • Forgetting your VAT number. Without it, your customer can't reclaim the VAT.

    This guide is general information, not tax advice. See GOV.UK's “VAT guide (VAT Notice 700)” for the full rules.

    Questions

    Do I have to send a VAT invoice?

    Yes, if you're VAT-registered and selling to another VAT-registered business, normally within 30 days. For sales to the public you only need to if they ask.

    Can I send a VAT invoice as a PDF?

    Yes. Electronic invoices are fine as long as your customer agrees and you keep a copy for your records.

    What is the VAT rate in the UK?

    The standard rate is 20%. Some goods and services are 5% (reduced rate) or 0% (zero rate).
