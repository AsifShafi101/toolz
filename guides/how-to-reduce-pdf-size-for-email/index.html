    How to reduce a PDF's size so you can email it

    Updated 6 November 2026 · etoolbox guides

    “Attachment too large” usually means a PDF full of photos or scanned pages. Here's how big is too big, and how to fix it.

    How big can an email attachment be?

    • Gmail: 25 MB in total per email.
    • Outlook: 20 MB for many accounts; work Exchange accounts are often 10 MB.
    • The hidden catch: email encoding makes files about a third bigger in transit, so a 20 MB PDF can bounce from a 25 MB limit.

    To be safe, aim for under 10 MB, especially when you don't know your recipient's email system.

    Why PDFs get so big

    Text takes almost no space. Large PDFs are nearly always caused by high-resolution images: phone photos, scans saved at 600 dpi, or slides with big pictures. Shrinking those images is what brings the size down.

    4 ways to make it smaller

    1. Compress it. Our PDF compressor shrinks the images inside your PDF. Try “Recommended” first, and “Strong” if it's still too big. Scans often drop by 70–90%.
    2. Remove pages you don't need with Split PDF, keeping only the relevant pages.
    3. Send it in parts. Split a long document into two emails.
    4. Rescan at a lower setting. 150–200 dpi in greyscale is plenty for most documents.

    Still too big?

    Share it with a link instead: Gmail offers Google Drive and Outlook offers OneDrive for large files. Check who can open the link before you send it.

    Questions

    Will compressing a PDF make the text blurry?

    No. Text stays sharp because it isn't an image. Only photos and scanned pages are reduced, and “Recommended” keeps them easy to read.

    Is it safe to compress private documents?

    With etoolbox, yes: the PDF is processed on your own device and never uploaded.

    What's the best size for email?

    Under 10 MB is safe for almost everyone. Under 20 MB usually works for Gmail-to-Gmail.
