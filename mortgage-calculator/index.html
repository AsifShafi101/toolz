Mortgage repayment calculator

Work out monthly mortgage payments, your loan-to-value (LTV) and the total interest, for repayment or interest-only mortgages.

Calculated on your device. Figures are rounded to the nearest penny.

Good to know

  • A lower LTV (bigger deposit) usually gets you better rates. 60%, 75% and 90% are common cut-offs.
  • This assumes the same rate for the whole term. Most UK deals are fixed for 2–5 years and then move to the lender's standard variable rate.
  • It doesn't include fees, Stamp Duty or insurance.

Questions

Repayment or interest only?

With a repayment mortgage you clear the loan by the end of the term. With interest only, you still owe the full amount at the end and need a plan to repay it.
