Case converter

Change text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, snake_case or kebab-case in one click.

When to use each

  • Sentence case: fixes text typed with Caps Lock on.
  • Title Case: headings, product names and blog titles. Small words like 'and' and 'of' stay lowercase.
  • kebab-case: web addresses (slugs), such as /summer-sale-2026.

Questions

Does it work with accents?

Yes. Letters like é, ü and ñ are converted correctly.

Is my text stored?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
