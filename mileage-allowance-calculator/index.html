    HMRC approved mileage rates 2026/27

    • Cars and vans: 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in the tax year, then 25p. The rate went up from 45p on 6 April 2026, the first rise since 2011.
    • Motorcycles: 24p a mile. Bicycles: 20p a mile.
    • Passengers: up to 5p per passenger per mile can be paid tax-free.

    If your employer pays less than the approved rate, you can claim Mileage Allowance Relief on the difference from HMRC. Claims for 2025/26 and earlier use the old 45p rate.

    Questions

    Does the 55p rate apply to electric cars?

    Yes. For your own car, the same rates apply whether it's petrol, diesel, hybrid or electric.

    Can self-employed people use these rates?

    Yes. Sole traders can use simplified expenses at the same per-mile rates instead of claiming actual vehicle costs.
