    Word to PDF converter

    Convert a Word document (.docx) to PDF in your browser, with headings, lists, tables and images. There's no upload and no sign-up.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Your browser's print window opens. Choose Save as PDF as the destination. On a phone, use Share → Print → pinch out to save.

    Your document preview will appear here.

    Good to know

    • Headings, bold and italic text, lists, tables, links and images are kept.
    • Complex layouts (text boxes, columns, headers and footers) are simplified.
    • Old .doc files: open them in Word and save as .docx first.

    Questions

    Is my document uploaded?

    No. The conversion runs in your browser, which is ideal for contracts, CVs and anything confidential.

    Can I convert Google Docs?

    Yes. In Google Docs, go to File → Download → PDF. Or download as .docx and use this tool.
