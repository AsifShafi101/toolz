      Tips for meta descriptions that get clicks

      • Aim for 120–160 characters. Google cuts off longer ones at around 920 pixels on desktop.
      • Include your main keyword. Google highlights it in bold when it matches the search.
      • Say what makes you different, and end with a clear action.
      • Write a unique description for every page.

      Google sometimes rewrites descriptions using text from your page, so make sure your page content is clear too.

      Questions

      Do meta descriptions help my ranking?

      Not directly. A good description gets more people to click your result, and that brings more visitors.

      Where do I paste the code?

      In the section of your page. In WordPress, Wix or Squarespace, use the page's SEO settings instead.
