    Questions

    Will PNG make my photo better quality?

    No. It keeps the current quality exactly, but files are usually larger. Use PNG when you need to edit the image further or need a format that won't lose detail.

    Will the background become transparent?

    No. JPGs have no transparency to keep. Use the background remover to make backgrounds transparent.
