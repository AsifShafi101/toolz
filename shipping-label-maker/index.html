    How to make a shipping label

    1. Paste the address from an order email or message, or type it in. We'll split it into name, street, town and postcode for you.
    2. Add your return address once. It's remembered on this device for next time.
    3. Pick extras if you need them: a service note like Tracked 48, an order number barcode, the number of parcels or the weight. Every label is stamped with the date and time it was made.
    4. Download the PDF and print it on a 4×6 label, or on A6 or 100×150 mm labels.

    Printing tips

    • Thermal label printers (Zebra, Rollo, MUNBYN, DYMO 4XL and others): choose the 4×6 paper size and print at 100%. The label is black and white at 300 dpi, so it prints sharp.
    • Normal printers: print on A6 sticky labels, or on plain paper and tape it to the parcel with clear tape over the whole label.
    • Put the label on the largest flat side of the parcel, away from seams and edges.

    Is this a postage label?

    This tool makes the address label. It doesn't include paid postage. Buy postage from your carrier, for example Royal Mail Click & Drop, Parcelforce, Evri or DPD, or use stamps: tick Leave space for stamps and stick them in the box.

    Questions

    Does the label include postage?

    No. It's an address label. Buy postage from Royal Mail, Evri, DPD or another carrier, or use stamps and tick “Leave space for stamps”.

    What size is a 4×6 label?

    4 × 6 inches, which is 101.6 × 152.4 mm. It's the standard size for thermal label printers. We also make 100 × 150 mm and A6 (105 × 148 mm) labels.

    Can I print several copies?

    Yes. Set the number of copies and the PDF has one label per page, ready for a roll of labels.

    Is my customer's address safe?

    Yes. The label is drawn in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored on our servers. Only your own return address is remembered, and only on your device.

    Why is there a date and time on the label?

    It shows when the parcel was labelled, which helps when you send a lot of parcels or need to check when something went out. Pro users can change the date and time or leave it off.

    What is the barcode for?

    If you add an order or reference number, we print it as a Code 128 barcode, so you or your courier can scan it to match the parcel to the order.
