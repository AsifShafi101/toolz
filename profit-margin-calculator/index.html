Margin vs markup

Margin is profit as a percentage of the selling price. Markup is profit as a percentage of the cost. Something that costs £6 and sells for £10 makes £4 profit. That's a 40% margin and a 66.67% markup.

Formulas

  • Margin = (price − cost) ÷ price × 100
  • Markup = (price − cost) ÷ cost × 100
  • Price for a target margin = cost ÷ (1 − margin)

Tip: if you're VAT registered, use prices without VAT, because the VAT isn't your money.

Questions

What's a good profit margin?

It varies widely by sector. Food and retail often run on thin margins, while services and digital products can be much higher. Compare yourself with businesses like yours.

Why can't the margin be 100% or more?

Margin is a share of the selling price, so even with zero cost it can only approach 100%. Markup, however, can be any size.
