    Quote vs estimate

    A quote is a fixed price for the job. An estimate is your best guess and can change. Make clear which one you're giving, and say what's included and excluded.

    Tips for winning more work

    • Break the price into clear lines (labour, materials, extras).
    • Add a 'valid until' date so prices don't drift.
    • State your deposit and payment terms up front.
