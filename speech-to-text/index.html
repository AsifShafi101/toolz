Speech to text (dictation)

Speak and watch your words appear as text. It's great for notes, emails and documents. Copy the transcript or download it as a text file.

Tips

  • Speak clearly at a normal pace in a quiet room.
  • Say punctuation out loud in some browsers, e.g. "comma" or "full stop".
  • Works best in Chrome, Edge and Safari.

Privacy: speech recognition is provided by your browser. In Chrome and Edge, audio is sent to Google or Microsoft to turn it into text. We never receive or store it.

Questions

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, in Safari. You can also use the microphone key on your phone's keyboard in any app.

Is there a time limit?

No. Keep talking and it keeps listening. Click Stop when you're done.
