How to share your Wi-Fi with a QR code

  1. Enter your network name exactly as it appears on your phone, including capital letters.
  2. Choose the security type. Most routers use WPA2 or WPA3, which is the default.
  3. Enter the password and test the code with your phone camera.
  4. Print it for the counter, tables or reception.

Keep your business network safe

Share a separate guest network, not the one your till or office computers use. Most broadband routers let you turn on a guest network in their settings.

Questions

Is my Wi-Fi password sent anywhere?

No. The code is made entirely in your browser, and your password never leaves your device.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. iPhones (iOS 11 and later) and modern Android phones join Wi-Fi straight from the camera app.

I changed my Wi-Fi password. Does the code still work?

No. The password is built into the code, so make a new code whenever you change it.
