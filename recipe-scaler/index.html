    Scaled ingredients

    Baking tip: cups measure volume, so 1 cup of flour (about 125 g) weighs much less than 1 cup of sugar (about 200 g). For precise baking, weigh dry ingredients.

    Questions

    What about eggs?

    Scaled eggs can come out as fractions (e.g. 1½). Round to the nearest whole egg, or beat one egg and use part of it.
