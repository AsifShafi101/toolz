    Add a watermark to a PDF

    Stamp CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, your name or your logo on every page of a PDF. Choose tiled or single, angle, size and transparency, and flatten it so it can't be removed. Free, and your PDF never leaves your device.

      How to watermark a PDF

      1. Choose your PDF.
      2. Type your text (or pick CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT…) or upload your logo.
      3. Set the position, angle, size and opacity. The preview updates as you go.
      4. Click Add watermark and download your PDF.

      Make the watermark hard to remove

      A normal watermark is added as a separate layer, which someone with a PDF editor could delete. For contracts, proofs and price lists you're sending out, use Tiled so it covers the whole page and tick Flatten. Flattening turns each page into a high-resolution image with the watermark baked in, so it can't be selected or removed.

      Private by design

      Most PDF websites upload your files to their servers. etoolbox adds the watermark in your browser, so confidential documents never leave your device.

      Questions

      Is it free?

      Yes. There's no sign-up and no limit, and we don't add our own branding to your PDF.

      Can I use my company logo?

      Yes. Choose 'Logo / image' and upload a PNG, JPG or WebP. A PNG with a transparent background looks best.

      Can I watermark only some pages?

      Yes. Choose first page, odd or even pages, or type page numbers such as 1-3, 5.

      Does it work with rotated or landscape pages?

      Yes. The watermark is placed according to how each page is displayed.

      Will flattening make my file bigger?

      Usually yes, because each page becomes an image. Leave Flatten off if the file needs to stay small or the text needs to stay searchable.
