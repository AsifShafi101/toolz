    How to add it to your email

    • Gmail: Settings (⚙) → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new, then paste.
    • Outlook: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature, then paste.
    • Apple Mail: Mail → Settings → Signatures → +, untick 'Always match my default message font', then paste.

    UK limited companies should include their registered name, number and office address on business emails.

    Questions

    How does the logo upload work?

    We resize your logo for email (sharp on retina screens) and host it on etoolbox so Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail can all display it. The image is public to anyone with its link, like any logo in an email, so only upload a logo you're allowed to use.

    My logo doesn't show for some recipients. Why?

    Some email apps hide images until the reader clicks 'Show images'. That's normal for every signature logo.

    Are my details saved?

    Only in this browser, so you can come back and edit them. Nothing is sent to us.
