    Why do DNS changes take time?

    Each DNS provider caches your records for the record's TTL (time to live). After you change a record, providers keep showing the old value until their cached copy expires, usually minutes and occasionally up to 48 hours. Lower the TTL a day before planned changes to make them spread faster.

    Questions

    What does ⚠️ mean?

    That provider is returning a different answer from most others, usually because it still has the old record cached.

    Which record do I need?

    A/AAAA point your domain at a website, MX handles email, TXT holds verification and SPF/DMARC, and CNAME is an alias for another name.
