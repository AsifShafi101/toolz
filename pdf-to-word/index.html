PDF to Word converter

Turn the text in a PDF into an editable Word document (.docx), with paragraphs and headings preserved. It's free, and your file never leaves your device.

    Result

    Your file will appear here, ready to download.

    What gets converted

    • ✓ All the text, grouped into paragraphs
    • ✓ Headings, which are detected from larger text and made bold
    • ✓ Page breaks
    • ✗ Exact layout, images and tables. For pixel-perfect layouts, a desktop app such as Microsoft Word (File → Open the PDF) does a fuller conversion.

    Scanned PDFs are just pictures of text, so there's no text to extract. They need OCR software.

    Private by design

    Most PDF websites upload your files to their servers. etoolbox processes them in your browser, so contracts, invoices and ID documents never leave your device.

    Questions

    Does it work with Google Docs?

    Yes. Upload the .docx to Google Drive and open it with Google Docs.

    Why is my document empty?

    If the PDF is a scan or photo, it contains images rather than text. You'd need OCR (text recognition) to convert it.
