    Stamp duty calculator

    Work out the stamp duty on a home in England or Northern Ireland, Land and Buildings Transaction Tax in Scotland, or Land Transaction Tax in Wales. Covers first-time buyers, home movers, second homes and buy-to-let, with a band-by-band breakdown.

    Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Rates checked on 24 November 2026 against GOV.UK (SDLT), gov.scot (LBTT 2026–27) and gov.wales (LTT). Always confirm with your solicitor or conveyancer.

    Stamp duty rates in England and Northern Ireland

    Portion of the priceStandardAdditional property
    Up to £125,0000%5%
    £125,001 – £250,0002%7%
    £250,001 – £925,0005%10%
    £925,001 – £1.5 million10%15%
    Over £1.5 million12%17%

    First-time buyers pay nothing up to £300,000 and 5% from £300,001 to £500,000. Above £500,000, standard rates apply to the whole price. Non-UK residents pay an extra 2%.

    Scotland (LBTT)

    0% up to £145,000 (£175,000 for first-time buyers), 2% to £250,000, 5% to £325,000, 10% to £750,000 and 12% above. Second homes pay an 8% Additional Dwelling Supplement on the whole price.

    Wales (LTT)

    0% up to £225,000, 6% to £400,000, 7.5% to £750,000, 10% to £1.5 million and 12% above. There's no first-time buyer relief. Additional properties pay higher rates, from 5% on the first £180,000.

    If you're replacing your main home but haven't sold the old one yet, you usually pay the higher rates and can claim a refund if you sell it within the time limit (usually 3 years). Shared ownership, mixed-use and non-residential purchases have different rules.

    Questions

    Do first-time buyers pay stamp duty?

    In England and Northern Ireland, not on homes up to £300,000. Between £300,000 and £500,000, you pay 5% on the part above £300,000. In Scotland, first-time buyers pay nothing up to £175,000; Wales has no special relief, but nothing is due below £225,000.

    How much is stamp duty on a £300,000 house?

    For a home mover in England: £5,000. For a first-time buyer: £0. For a second home: £20,000.

    When do I pay?

    Your solicitor or conveyancer usually pays it for you within 14 days of completion in England and Northern Ireland, and 30 days in Scotland and Wales.
