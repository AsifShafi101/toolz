    UK VAT calculator

    Add VAT to a price or work out how much VAT is included. It covers the 20% standard rate, the 5% reduced rate, or any rate you choose.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Calculated on your device. Figures are rounded to the nearest penny.

    How to work out VAT

    • Adding 20% VAT: multiply the net price by 1.2. For example, £100 + VAT = £120.
    • Removing 20% VAT: divide the gross price by 1.2. For example, £120 including VAT is £100 net, with £20 of VAT.
    • Common mistake: taking 20% off a VAT-inclusive price gives the wrong answer. To find the VAT in a gross price, divide by 6, not 5.

    UK VAT rates

    Most goods and services use the standard rate of 20%. Some, such as domestic energy and children's car seats, use the reduced 5% rate. Others, including most food and children's clothes, are zero-rated. Check GOV.UK for the rate that applies to what you sell.

    Questions

    How do I calculate 20% VAT?

    To add VAT, multiply by 1.2. To find the VAT inside a VAT-inclusive price, divide the price by 6. To get the price without VAT, divide by 1.2.

    When do I have to register for VAT?

    You must register when your VAT-taxable turnover goes over the registration threshold set by HMRC. Check GOV.UK for the current figure.

    Is this calculator accurate?

    Yes. It works to the penny and rounds the final figures to 2 decimal places, as invoices do.
