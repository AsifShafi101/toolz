      How to sign a PDF

      1. Choose your PDF. Its pages appear on the right.
      2. Draw your signature with a mouse or finger, type your name, or upload a photo of your signature.
      3. Click Place signature, then click where it should go on the page. Drag to move it and use the corner handle to resize.
      4. Add the date or initials if needed, then click Download signed PDF.

      Is an electronic signature legal in the UK?

      Yes. In England and Wales, electronic signatures are generally valid for most contracts and business documents, including tenancy agreements, employment contracts and supplier terms. Some documents still need a wet-ink or witnessed signature, such as wills and some deeds and property transfers. If you're unsure, check with the other party or a solicitor.

      Private by design

      Your document and signature stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

      Questions

      Can I sign on my phone?

      Yes. Draw your signature with your finger, then tap the page to place it.

      Can I sign more than one page?

      Yes. Click Place signature again and click on another page as many times as you need.

      Is my signature saved?

      No. It exists only in this browser tab and disappears when you close it.

      Is it free?

      Yes. No account, no limits and no watermark.
