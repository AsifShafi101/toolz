    Digital postcard maker

    Send a free online postcard from London, Paris, New York, Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and 21 more famous places, or use your own photo. Write your message, add who it's to and from, then share a link or download it. No sign-up, no ads in the card.

    How to send a digital postcard

    1. Pick a famous place, search for any place in the world, or upload your own holiday photo.
    2. Choose a design and colours. “Greetings from” and the place name appear on the front.
    3. Write your message and who it's to and from. You'll see it on the back of the card.
    4. Press Copy share link, or send it by WhatsApp or email. You can also download the card as a picture to post on social media or print.

    Why use this postcard maker?

    • Completely free: no membership, no “one free card a month” and no sign-up.
    • Private: your message travels inside the link, and your own photos never leave your device.
    • Looks like the real thing: a stamp, postmark and handwriting on the back, and it turns over like a real card.

    Any place in the world

    Type a city, country, landmark or even “Antarctica” into the search box. You'll see freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons, the free media library behind Wikipedia. Pick one and it goes straight onto your card.

    The photos

    The landmark photos are freely licensed pictures from Wikimedia Commons, taken by the photographers credited on each card. Most are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA). The credit printed on the back of every postcard keeps it in line with the licence.

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Make and send as many postcards as you like. There's no account and no paid tier for postcards.

    Can the person I send it to see it without an app?

    Yes. The link opens in any web browser on a phone or computer, and the card turns over to show your message.

    Can I use my own photo?

    Yes. Choose “Your own photo”. It stays on your device, so download the card and send it as a picture.

    Can I print it?

    Yes. Download both sides as images and print them at 6 × 4 inches (15 × 10 cm), standard postcard size.

    Who took the photos?

    Photographers who share their work on Wikimedia Commons. Each card shows the photographer and the licence on the back.
