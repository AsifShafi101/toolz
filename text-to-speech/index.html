Text to speech

Paste any text and listen to it read aloud. Choose British and other voices and adjust the speed and pitch. It's free and works in your browser.

Uses

  • Proofread your writing by ear and catch mistakes
  • Listen to articles and emails while you work
  • Help with reading, dyslexia and learning languages

Voices come from your device and browser. Edge and Chrome offer the most natural-sounding voices.

Questions

Can I download the audio as an MP3?

Browsers don't allow saving built-in voices as audio files. To record it, use your computer's sound recorder while it plays.

Is my text sent anywhere?

Most voices run on your device. Some 'online' voices (marked in the voice name) are provided by your browser's maker.
