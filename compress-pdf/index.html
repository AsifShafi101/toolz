Compress PDF

Make PDFs smaller so they fit email limits and upload forms. Choose how much to compress. Your file is processed on your device and never uploaded.

    'Recommended' and 'Strong' work best on scanned or image-heavy PDFs. They turn pages into images, so text can't be selected afterwards. 'Light' keeps everything editable.

    Result

    Your file will appear here, ready to download.

    Which level should I choose?

    • Light: tidies up the file's structure without touching quality. It's best for text documents, though savings are usually modest.
    • Recommended: 144 dpi, which looks sharp on screen and prints fine. It's great for scans and photos.
    • Strong: 96 dpi gives the smallest files and suits email attachments and upload limits.

    If compressing doesn't make the file smaller, we keep your original so you never get a bigger file.

    Private by design

    Most PDF websites upload your files to their servers. etoolbox processes them in your browser, so contracts, invoices and ID documents never leave your device.

    Questions

    Why didn't my PDF get much smaller?

    Text-only PDFs are already compact. The biggest savings come from scanned documents and PDFs full of photos.

    Can I still search the text?

    With 'Light', yes. 'Recommended' and 'Strong' turn pages into images, so the text can no longer be selected or searched.
