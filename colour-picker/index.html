Colour picker & converter

Pick a colour and get its HEX, RGB, HSL and CMYK codes, lighter and darker shades, and WCAG contrast scores. You can also pick colours from your screen or a photo.

Shades

Text contrast (WCAG)

Which code do I need?

  • HEX (#FF6B35): websites, Canva and most design apps.
  • RGB: screens and CSS.
  • CMYK: print shops and leaflets.

Contrast: for readable text, aim for at least 4.5:1 (WCAG AA). Large headings need 3:1.

Questions

How do I find a colour from my logo?

Click 'Pick from a photo', upload your logo, then click the colour you want.

Are CMYK values exact?

They're a standard conversion. For brand-critical printing, ask your printer for a proof, because inks and paper affect the result.
