    Photo metadata & GPS remover

    See the hidden information in your photos (GPS location, camera, date and time) and remove it before you share or sell online. JPEGs are cleaned without losing any quality.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Why remove photo metadata?

    Phones save the exact GPS location in most photos. If you post a photo of an item for sale, or of your home or children, people may be able to see where it was taken. Cleaning also removes the camera's serial number and the date and time.

    Many social networks remove location data when you upload, but email, WhatsApp documents, marketplaces and websites often keep it.

    Questions

    Does it reduce quality?

    No. For JPEG and PNG, only the metadata is removed and the image itself is kept byte-for-byte. Photos that need rotating are re-saved at very high quality.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. Everything happens on your device.
