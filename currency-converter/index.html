    Currency converter

    Convert pounds, euros, dollars and 30 currencies using the European Central Bank's daily reference rates. It's ideal for invoices, travel and pricing.

    🌐 Downloads exchange rates from the ECB (Frankfurter)

    About these rates

    These are the European Central Bank's official reference (mid-market) rates, published every working day. Banks, card providers and travel money shops add a margin, so the rate you're offered will be a little worse. For VAT on foreign-currency invoices, HMRC publishes its own monthly exchange rates.

    Questions

    Are the rates live?

    They're updated once every working day (around 4pm CET), which is right for invoices, quotes and planning, but not for trading.

    Where do the rates come from?

    The European Central Bank, via the free, open-source Frankfurter service.
