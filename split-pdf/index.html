    Split PDF

    Extract the pages you need, delete pages you don't, or split a PDF into separate files. It's free and private, because everything happens in your browser.

    🔒 Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

      Result

      Your file will appear here, ready to download.

      Page ranges

      • 1-3 means pages 1, 2 and 3.
      • 5 means page 5 only.
      • 8- means page 8 to the end.
      • Combine them with commas: 1-3, 5, 8-

      Private by design

      Most PDF websites upload your files to their servers. etoolbox processes them in your browser, so contracts, invoices and ID documents never leave your device.

      Questions

      Does splitting reduce quality?

      No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, without re-compressing anything.

      Can I reorder pages?

      Yes. When extracting, the pages come out in the order you type them, for example 3, 1, 2.
